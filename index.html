    About 3EYE Jackpot

    3EYE Jackpot is a football pool community. Members add an agreed share, the contributions are combined into one stake, and that stake enters a single football jackpot.

    Clear records

    Contributions and pool details are communicated clearly, and results are shared whether they go our way or not.

    Good to know

    • Nothing here is a guarantee of a win.
    • Bookmaker prizes are shared among all winners in a category, so payouts can vary.
    • Read our Play safe page.
    • Read the full Pool Terms before you contribute.

    Play safe

    18+ only. Betting should be fun, never a way to solve money problems.

    Ground rules

    • Only contribute money you can afford to lose. Jackpots are very hard to win.
    • Never borrow, or use rent, school fees or food money.
    • Set a limit for the week before you start, and stick to it.
    • Do not chase losses. Losing a pool does not make the next one more likely to win.

    Signs it may be becoming a problem

    • Spending more than you planned, or hiding how much you spend.
    • Feeling anxious or irritable when you are not betting.
    • Betting to win back what you lost.
    • Arguments with family or friends about money.

    Need a break?

    Message us on WhatsApp and ask for your account to be suspended. We will pause it and stop accepting contributions from you. You can also talk to someone you trust, or to a qualified counsellor or doctor.
