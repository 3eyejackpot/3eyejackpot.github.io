    About 3EYE Jackpot

    3EYE Jackpot is a football pool community. Members add an agreed share, the contributions are combined into one stake, and that stake enters a single football jackpot.

    3EYE Jackpot is a non-commercial group and is not affiliated with any bookmaker. A flat operating fee covers SMS, hosting and admin costs; nothing is taken from winnings.

    Clear records

    Contributions and pool details are communicated clearly, and results are shared whether they go our way or not.

    Play safe

    3EYE Jackpot is for adults aged 18 and over. Betting should be entertainment, never a source of income or a way to recover losses.

    Our commitment

    We run community pools for adults only. We encourage every member to take part within their means, and we will pause any account at the member’s request, without question.

    Set your limits

    • Decide how much you can afford to lose before you contribute, and keep to it.
    • Never use money meant for rent, school fees, food, bills or savings.
    • Do not borrow in order to bet.
    • Do not chase losses. A previous loss does not make the next jackpot more likely to win.
    • Remember that jackpots are very difficult to win and that no result is guaranteed.

    Know the warning signs

    • Spending more money or time on betting than you intended.
    • Hiding your betting or your losses from family and friends.
    • Feeling anxious, restless or irritable when you are not betting.
    • Betting to win back what you have lost.
    • Disagreements at home or at work because of money or betting.

    If you need a break

    If you have an account, you can pause it yourself in My Account under Settings. Or message us on WhatsApp and ask for it to be suspended. Either way, we stop accepting contributions from you until you ask us to reopen it.

    Where to get support

    If betting is affecting your health, finances or relationships, speak to a trusted friend or family member, or to a qualified counsellor or doctor. You do not have to deal with it alone.
